Everything you need to know

How does Aurum Capital work?

Invest across crypto, equities, commodities and real estate from one dashboard, with P&L analytics updated every 30 seconds.

Is my investment capital secure?

256-bit AES encryption, mandatory two-factor authentication, cold storage for digital assets and regular third-party audits.

What assets can I invest in?

Crypto (50+ listed coins), global equities, commodities such as gold, silver and crude oil, and fractional real estate.

What are the minimum investment amounts?

No minimum for crypto or stocks. Real estate starts from $1,000.

How do deposits and withdrawals work?

Deposit by bank transfer, card or crypto. Bank withdrawals take 1–3 business days, crypto about 24 hours, with no withdrawal fees on standard accounts.

Are there management or trading fees?

No annual management fee. A small trading spread is shown before you confirm any trade.

Under 5 minutes. Identity checks are instant for most applicants.

Yes — a live dashboard shows value, unrealised P&L and allocation breakdown.
