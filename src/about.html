About Aurum Capital

One account for crypto, shares, commodities and real estate

Aurum Capital is an online investment platform. You open an account, add funds, and buy or sell assets at live market prices — all from one dashboard on your phone or computer.

What you can invest in

₿CryptocurrenciesBitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB, XRP, Cardano and Litecoin.
ASharesApple, Nvidia, Microsoft, Tesla, Amazon and Alphabet.
AuCommoditiesGold, silver and crude oil.
Real-estate listingsUnits in listed properties, with a lock-in period you choose.

You can also open leveraged positions (CFDs) on the Trade page. Leverage magnifies gains and losses — read Risks before using it.

How it works

  1. Create an account with your email address and confirm it.
  2. Add funds by crypto transfer or PayPal. Deposits are credited once our team confirms the payment.
  3. Invest from $10. Every order shows the price and fee before you confirm, and is priced and settled on our servers at the live market price.
  4. Track your portfolio, profit and loss in real time, and withdraw to cash whenever you choose. Withdrawal requests are reviewed before they’re paid.

What we commit to

Clear prices and fees. Prices come from market data providers and refresh every 30 seconds. Our charges are listed on the Fees page and shown before every order.

No promised returns. We don’t promise or target any return. Markets move up and down, and you may get back less than you invest.

Help when you need it. Our support team is available through live chat and email from the Contact page.
