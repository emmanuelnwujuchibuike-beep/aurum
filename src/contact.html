Common questions

How long does account verification take?

KYC verification is typically completed within 1–4 hours; complex cases may take up to 24 hours. You’ll get an email as soon as your account is verified and ready to trade.

What is the minimum investment amount?

The minimum position is $10.

How are my funds protected?

Your account is protected by your password, and all connections to Aurum Capital are encrypted (HTTPS). Balances and orders are processed on our servers, and withdrawals are reviewed before they are paid. Investing still carries risk — see Risks.

Can I withdraw my funds at any time?

Cash balances and crypto holdings can be withdrawn at any time, typically within 1–3 business days. Real estate positions have a standard 30-day notice period.

What are Aurum’s fees?

A 0.5% trading fee on crypto and equity positions; real estate carries a 1% annual management fee. No deposit fees; withdrawal fees vary by method.

Do you offer white-glove wealth management?

Yes. Aurum Pro provides a dedicated relationship manager, a personalised strategy, priority execution and access to off-market opportunities. Minimum portfolio value for Pro is $250,000.
