How to invest in shares

What a share is, what moves its price, and how to get started without taking on more risk than you need.

Aurum Capital · 29 October 2026 · General information, not personal advice

What a share is

A share is a small piece of ownership in a company. If the company grows its profits over time, its shares tend to become more valuable; if it struggles, they can fall a long way. Some companies also pay part of their profits to shareholders as dividends.

What moves share prices

Company results, expectations about future profits, interest rates, the wider economy and investor mood all play a part. Even large, well-known companies can lose a third or more of their value in a bad year.

Investing versus trading

Investing usually means buying and holding for years, accepting ups and downs along the way. Trading means buying and selling over days or hours to profit from short-term moves. Trading is harder, costs more in fees, and most people who trade frequently do worse than those who hold.

Starting small

  • You can buy from $10, so you don’t need a whole share.
  • Spreading money across several companies — and other asset classes — reduces the damage any single company can do.
  • Check the fee on the order screen before you confirm; frequent small trades add up.
